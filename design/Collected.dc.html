gathered in the sound of starlight

Music begins as starlight, gathered into sound. The ancients called it the music of the spheres: the hidden harmony that keeps the planets turning and every living thing in tune. Each song draws on it through the same formula, the spark, the shaping, the fire and the release, and every page of brodband holds one of its verses. Here they are gathered together, made to keep, wear and display, so a little of that harmony can travel with you.

the formula, in sixteen verses

Each engraving is one verse of the same story. The first tone sets the cosmic symphony in motion, and a spark catches. A lantern projects the song into moving pictures, a serpent turns it through endless cycles of time, many streams flow as one river, and one vibration awakens another.
