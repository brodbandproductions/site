Founder · Composer · Producer · Engineer · Performer

alex brodsky

brodband productions is the creative home of Alex Brodsky: one hub under his name for a broad range of sounds, skills and projects.

Alex fell in love with the guitar as a seven-year-old boy, when he first picked up an acoustic and began his classical training. By the age of ten he was performing on stage with the youth centre band in his home city of Kiev, and the pull of the stage never left him. After his family moved to Canada in 1980 he expanded his world into keyboards, synthesizers and programming. He went on to play rock, pop and electronica to sold-out crowds in musical variety shows and clubs. As a founding member of the Edmonton electronic band Voice Industrie he performed everywhere from Moshfest at the Butterdome to an immersive dome concert at the Zeidler Star Theatre.

Today

shaping the sound, guiding the vision

Variety keeps the music alive. Alex writes original songs and co-writes with other artists. He builds full arrangements around the work of singer-songwriters and records, produces and films projects across many styles. Some are his own and many are shared, because the best music is rarely made alone. Every collaboration brings new voices and fresh ideas into the room. The joy is in creating something together and sharing it with the world, so that joy keeps spreading.

Studied: Synthesizer Programming and Performance Techniques and Live Sound Reinforcement at MacEwan University (then Grant MacEwan College), and Pro Tools certification through Berklee.
