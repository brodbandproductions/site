shaping notes and patterns into form

Where music begins, how it takes shape, and what it can become.

every song begins as a spark

A melody that arrives out of nowhere. A rhythm that will not let go. A song still waiting for its voice. At brodband productions those first sparks are shaped and refined into finished music, live sessions and visual stories, from the first note to the final frame.

What we can make together

from raw spark to finished form

  1. voice and guitar →full arrangement

    Bring us a song in its simplest form, a voice with a guitar or piano. We build the arrangement around it with drums, bass, keys, strings, horns and textures, played and programmed in the studio until the song sounds the way you hear it.

  2. lyric or riff →co-written song

    Start with a few lines or a riff that won’t let go. We write with you, shaping melody, chords, structure and words, until the pieces become one finished song that is still yours.

  3. finished song →animated music video

    We build a visual world for your song, scene by scene and in time with the beat, guided by its story and mood, and edit it into a music video ready for YouTube and social media.

  4. a great jam →filmed live session

    We record your band or project live, on multiple cameras and multitrack audio, then mix and edit the session so it keeps the heat of the moment.

  5. rough demo →release-ready track

    Send us your demo or session files. We record what’s missing, then produce, edit and mix until the track is ready for release.

  6. an open door →something else in mind?

    Soundtracks, sound design, live sound or a collaboration nobody has thought of yet. Tell us where you want to take your project, and we’ll find the way together.
